Looking forward to hearing all the great ideas at the CSTA New York Fall Conference but just can’t
sit still? 

Want to help a child in distress by helping crowd-knit and crowd-crochet blankets that 
we’ll donate to [Project Linus](https://www.projectlinus.org/)?

Join us at the Pickcode table in the vendors area. 

We will teach you how to knit or crochet, give you the yarn and the needles  and then set you loose to enjoy the conference. 

If you already knit or crochet, come by the Pickcode table, grab supplies, get back to the conference and knit away. 

We will also be collecting pre-made 7” X 9” blocks in any color acrylic yarn. The pre-made block will join other random blocks in a scrapy-happy blanket!

More information available at: [https://blanket-statement.github.io](https://blanket-statement.github.io)
